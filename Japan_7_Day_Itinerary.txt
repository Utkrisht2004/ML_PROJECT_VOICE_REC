7-DAY JAPAN ITINERARY

Days 1-3: TOKYO
- Arrive and explore Shibuya and Harajuku districts
- Visit Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa
- Explore Tokyo Skytree and Akihabara
- Experience Tsukiji Outer Market
- Visit Meiji Shrine and Shinjuku Gardens

Days 4-6: KYOTO
- Take bullet train to Kyoto
- Visit Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)
- Explore Arashiyama Bamboo Grove
- See Fushimi Inari Shrine
- Visit Kiyomizu-dera Temple
- Experience Gion district

Day 7: TOKYO
- Return to Tokyo
- Last-minute shopping in Ginza
- Depart from Tokyo

Travel Tips:
- Get a Japan Rail Pass before arrival
- Book accommodations in advance
- Download offline maps
- Learn basic Japanese phrases
